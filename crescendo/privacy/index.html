SuiteForge/Crescendo/Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Crescendo · Last updated 31 October 2026

The short version. Crescendo stores your data on your device, optionally synced through your own private iCloud. There is no Crescendo account. We do not use analytics or advertising trackers, and we never sell your data. Purchases are handled by Apple; nothing else leaves your device.

1. Who we are

Crescendo is an iPhone app published by Nicolas Dojcilovic under the name SuiteForge, who is the data controller for the processing described below (see the legal notice). For any privacy question, contact us at suiteforgeops@gmail.com.

2. Data stored on your device

Everything you create in Crescendo is stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device frameworks. If you enable iCloud, this data may sync through your own private Apple iCloud account, which SuiteForge cannot access. We do not operate any server that stores your personal content.

Apple Watch

During a workout, your iPhone and your Apple Watch exchange the workout and the sets you log directly between your own devices, through Apple's WatchConnectivity framework. Nothing goes through a SuiteForge server.

Apple Intelligence (optional)

If you describe in a sentence how to adapt today's workout, the sentence is analysed on your iPhone by Apple's on-device language model. It is never sent to SuiteForge, to Apple's servers or to any third party.

Import, export and erase

Importing a workout history reads a file you choose, on your iPhone. Exporting creates a CSV or JSON file that you save or share yourself. Settings › Data also lets you erase all your data.

3. Permissions Crescendo may request

Apple Health, on Apple Watch (optional)

When you start a workout, Crescendo can start a strength-training workout on your Apple Watch. With your permission, the Watch saves the workout, your heart rate and your active energy to Apple Health, and reads them to show your heart rate during the workout and the calories of the session. This data stays in Apple Health on your devices and is never sent to SuiteForge.

Notifications (optional)

A notification with a sound at the end of each rest period during a workout, scheduled locally on your iPhone. Crescendo sends no other notifications.

Each permission is optional, is requested only when relevant, and can be changed at any time in the iOS Settings app.

4. Subscriptions and purchases

Crescendo offers an optional auto-renewing subscription and an optional one-time lifetime purchase. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple. Crescendo checks your purchases on your device with Apple's StoreKit framework and uses no third-party purchase service: no purchase information is sent to SuiteForge or to anyone other than Apple. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not require or create an account.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data.

6. Your rights

Because your content is stored on your own device, you remain in control of it at all times: you can edit or delete it within the app, and removing the app removes its local data. As Crescendo is published from France, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies to the limited purchase-related processing described above; you may exercise your applicable rights of access, rectification, erasure and objection by contacting suiteforgeops@gmail.com.

7. Children

Crescendo is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new "last updated" date.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email suiteforgeops@gmail.com.
