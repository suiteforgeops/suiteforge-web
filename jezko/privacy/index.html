Privacy Policy

Ježko · Last updated 29 October 2026

The short version. Ježko stores your data on your device only. It does not sync to any cloud. There is no Ježko account. We do not use analytics or advertising trackers, and we never sell your data. Purchases are handled by Apple; nothing else leaves your device.

1. Who we are

Ježko is an iPhone app published by Nicolas Dojcilovic under the name SuiteForge, who is the data controller for the processing described below (see the legal notice). For any privacy question, contact us at suiteforgeops@gmail.com.

2. Data stored on your device

Everything you create in Ježko is stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device frameworks. Ježko does not sync your data to iCloud or to any other cloud service, and we do not operate any server that stores your personal content.

Profiles and recordings

Profile names, progress, review cards and voice recordings are stored only on your device. They are never sent to SuiteForge. A recording leaves your device only if you choose to share it yourself, through the iOS share sheet (on a child profile, behind a parental gate).

3. Permissions Ježko may request

Microphone (optional)

Used only when the learner taps the microphone button, to record a line and compare it with the model, or to say a sentence in Challenge mode. Recordings are stored on your device and are never uploaded. You can refuse and keep using the app: any spoken step can be skipped.

Speech recognition (optional)

In Challenge mode, Ježko recognises the words you say to award stars. Recognition runs entirely on your iPhone, with Apple's on-device speech framework. Your voice and the recognised words are never sent to SuiteForge, to Apple or to any third party, and Ježko uses no third-party AI service.

Notifications (optional)

A daily reminder for the lesson, if you turn it on. It is scheduled locally on your iPhone.

Each permission is optional, is requested only when relevant, and can be changed at any time in the iOS Settings app.

4. Subscriptions and purchases

Ježko offers an optional auto-renewing subscription and an optional one-time lifetime purchase. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple. Ježko checks your purchases on your device with Apple's StoreKit framework and uses no third-party purchase service: no purchase information is sent to SuiteForge or to anyone other than Apple. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not require or create an account.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data.

6. Your rights

Because your content is stored on your own device, you remain in control of it at all times: you can edit or delete it within the app, and removing the app removes its local data. As Ježko is published from France, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies to the limited purchase-related processing described above; you may exercise your applicable rights of access, rectification, erasure and objection by contacting suiteforgeops@gmail.com.

7. Children

Ježko is designed to be used by children together with their parents. We do not collect personal data from anyone, including children: profile names, recordings and progress are stored only on your device and are never sent to SuiteForge. Ježko contains no advertising, no third-party analytics, no chat and no social features.

On a child profile, purchases, links that leave the app and sharing are protected by a parental gate. Purchases are made by the adult who holds the Apple Account; the purchase data described in section 4 relates to that account, not to the child. If you have any question about your child's use of Ježko, contact us at suiteforgeops@gmail.com.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new "last updated" date.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email suiteforgeops@gmail.com.
