What it does

Journeys on real maps

Apple Health (read steps and walking distance)

With your permission, WalkQuest reads your step count and walking distance from Apple Health to move you along your journey and show your progress. It never writes to Health or reads anything else from it, and you can revoke access in iOS Settings at any time.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is WalkQuest free?

Yes. WalkQuest is free to download, with two full journeys (Paris to Versailles and the Promenade des Anglais), your daily goal, a streak with rest days, 3 challenges, 3 trophies and 30 days of history. Premium unlocks the five other journeys, every challenge and trophy, 12 months of history and the Lock Screen and journey widgets: a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase, shareable with up to 6 people through Family Sharing.

How do virtual walking journeys work?

WalkQuest adds up the steps and walking distance your iPhone already records in Apple Health and moves you along a real route on the map. You walk wherever you are; the journey advances stop after stop, with a celebration each time you reach one.

Do I need GPS or an Apple Watch?

No. WalkQuest never uses your location. It reads the steps and distance your iPhone records on its own; an Apple Watch simply makes the counts more accurate.

How is WalkQuest different from Pedometer++, StepsApp or Pacer?

WalkQuest turns your steps into journeys on real maps. According to their App Store listings, WalkQuest has no ads and never uses your location, while Pedometer++, StepsApp and Pacer contain ads and may use your location when the app isn't open; StepsApp and Pacer also use data to track you. WalkQuest's in-app purchases are listed from $2.99 to $14.99. Pedometer++, StepsApp and Pacer offer an Apple Watch app; WalkQuest uses the steps your iPhone and Apple Watch already record in Apple Health.

Does WalkQuest sell my activity data?

No. WalkQuest never sells or shares your activity data. Your steps and distance are read from Apple Health and never leave your iPhone.
