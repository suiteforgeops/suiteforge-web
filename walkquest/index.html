Apple Health (read steps)

With your permission, WalkQuest reads your step count from Apple Health to show your daily progress. It never writes to or reads anything else from Health, and you can revoke access in iOS Settings at any time.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is WalkQuest free?

Yes — WalkQuest is free to download with free daily step tracking. An optional subscription unlocks long-term trends and extra goals.

What's a good private step tracker for iPhone?

WalkQuest is a privacy-first step tracker: it reads steps from Apple Health on your device, has no account, no analytics and no trackers, and keeps your data local.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. WalkQuest uses the step data your iPhone already records through Apple Health. An Apple Watch simply adds more accurate counts.

How is WalkQuest different from Pedometer++, StepsApp or Pacer?

According to their App Store listings, WalkQuest has no ads and never uses your location, while Pedometer++, StepsApp and Pacer contain ads and may use your location when the app isn't open; StepsApp and Pacer also use data to track you. WalkQuest's in-app purchases are listed from $2.99 to $14.99. Pedometer++, StepsApp and Pacer offer an Apple Watch app; WalkQuest uses the steps your iPhone and Apple Watch already record in Apple Health.

Does WalkQuest sell my activity data?

No. WalkQuest never sells or shares your activity data. Your steps stay on your device.
