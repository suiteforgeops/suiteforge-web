Questions

Frequently asked

Is RoutineFlow free?

Yes — RoutineFlow is free to download with a free core routine. An optional subscription unlocks multiple routines and advanced options.

What's a good private morning routine app?

RoutineFlow is designed for a calm, private start to the day: no account, no analytics, no trackers, and your routines stay on your own device.

Can I have more than one routine?

You can build a morning routine for free; additional routines (evening, weekend, focus) are part of the optional subscription.

Does RoutineFlow track me?

No. There are no analytics or advertising trackers. Your routine data never leaves your device unless you use private iCloud sync.
