Privacy Policy

FastClear · Last updated 29 May 2026

The short version. FastClear stores your data on your device, optionally synced through your own private iCloud. There is no FastClear account. We do not use analytics or advertising trackers, and we never sell your data. The only information that leaves your device is the anonymous purchase data needed to manage an optional subscription.

1. Who we are

FastClear is an iPhone app published by SuiteForge. For any privacy question, contact us at suiteforgeops@gmail.com.

2. Data stored on your device

Everything you create in FastClear is stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device frameworks. If you enable iCloud, this data may sync through your own private Apple iCloud account, which SuiteForge cannot access. We do not operate any server that stores your personal content.

3. Permissions FastClear may request

Apple Health (write only)

If you allow it, FastClear writes your completed fasting periods to Apple Health. FastClear never reads other health data. This is optional and can be turned off in iOS Settings at any time.

Notifications

Used only to remind you when a fasting or eating window starts or ends. Optional.

Each permission is optional, is requested only when relevant, and can be changed at any time in the iOS Settings app.

4. Subscriptions and purchases

FastClear offers an optional auto-renewing subscription. Purchases are processed by Apple. To deliver and restore purchases across your devices, we use RevenueCat, Inc. as a service provider. For this purpose only, an anonymous app-user identifier together with purchase and receipt information is processed by Apple and RevenueCat. This information is not linked to your personal content in the app and is not used for advertising. See Apple's Privacy Policy and RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not require or create an account.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data.

6. Your rights

Because your content is stored on your own device, you remain in control of it at all times: you can edit or delete it within the app, and removing the app removes its local data. As FastClear is published from France, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies to the limited purchase-related processing described above; you may exercise your applicable rights of access, rectification, erasure and objection by contacting suiteforgeops@gmail.com.

7. Children

FastClear is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new "last updated" date.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email suiteforgeops@gmail.com.
