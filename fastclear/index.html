What it does

Everything you need, nothing you don't.

Start when you really started

Forgot to start the timer? Say when you last ate (now, an hour ago, or any time) and your fast starts from that moment. Correct it later if you need to.

A goal you can go past

Your goal is a milestone, not a stop: the fast keeps going until you end it, and the time beyond the goal is counted. Then the eating window opens and counts down.

On your lock screen

A free Live Activity and Dynamic Island show your fast as it goes, with an End button. Interactive widgets, a Control Center control and Siri start or end a fast without opening the app.

Honest by design

Milestones like ketosis are shown as estimates, with their sources. No weight-loss promises, a free version you can keep using, and your history exportable anytime.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is FastClear free?

Yes. Every protocol, the Live Activity, 30 days of history and the export of your data are free, with no time limit. Premium adds your full history, in-depth statistics and the widgets, as a monthly or yearly subscription (7-day free trial on the yearly plan) or a one-time lifetime purchase.

Can I change the start time of a fast?

Yes. You can start a fast from when you last ate, correct the start of the fast in progress, and add or edit past fasts in your history.

What's the best app for private intermittent fasting?

FastClear is built for people who want a private fasting timer: it has no account, no analytics and no trackers, its App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected", and every fast is stored on your own device rather than on a company server.

Does FastClear need an internet connection?

No. FastClear works fully offline. A connection is only used by Apple to manage an optional purchase.

Does FastClear work with Apple Health?

No. FastClear 2.0 doesn't read or write Apple Health: your fasting history stays in the app, on your device, and you can export it at any time.

How is FastClear different from Zero, Fastic or BodyFast?

FastClear is a fasting timer only, built for privacy: its App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected", it has no ads and no account, and its in-app purchases are listed from $0.99 to $9.99 with a lifetime option. Zero, Fastic and BodyFast go further with food and calorie tracking (and an Apple Watch app for Zero), but according to their App Store listings they collect data linked to your identity, including health and fitness data for Zero and Fastic, and Fastic and BodyFast use data to track you.
