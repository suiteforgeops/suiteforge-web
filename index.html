On your device

Your fasts, routines, steps, contacts and lessons live on your iPhone, or in your own private iCloud. Never on our servers.

The one exception: to manage an optional subscription, Apple and our payment provider process an anonymous purchase identifier. It is never linked to your personal content, and each app's privacy policy spells this out in plain language.

Questions

Good to know

Does SuiteForge collect my personal data?

No. SuiteForge apps store everything on your device (some can also sync through your own private iCloud). There are no SuiteForge accounts, no analytics SDKs and no third-party trackers. The only data that leaves your device is the anonymous purchase information Apple and our subscription provider need to manage a subscription.

Do I need an account?

No. SuiteForge apps work immediately on first launch: no sign-up, no email, no password.

What kinds of apps does SuiteForge make?

Focused, single-purpose iPhone apps: Health & Wellness (intermittent fasting, morning routines, step tracking), Productivity (KeepContact, a private personal CRM) and Education (Ježko, Serbian for kids and families). Each app does one thing well and runs on-device.

Are the apps free?

Each app is free to download, with an optional subscription that unlocks advanced features; some also offer a one-time lifetime purchase. You can use the core of every app without paying.

Do you make apps for children?

Yes: Ježko teaches Serbian to children aged 6 to 14 and their families. Like every SuiteForge app, it has no ads, no account and no tracking, and purchases and external links sit behind a parental gate on a child profile.

How do I get help or suggest a feature?

Every app has a feedback option in its settings, or you can email suiteforgeops@gmail.com directly. We read everything.
