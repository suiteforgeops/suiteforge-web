Why privacy-first

Your life is yours. Not a dataset.

Stays on your device

Your fasts, routines, steps and contacts live on your iPhone — and, if you choose, in your own private iCloud. Never on our servers.

No accounts, ever

No sign-up, no email, no password. Open the app and start. There is no SuiteForge login because there is nothing for us to log in to.

No tracking

No analytics SDKs, no advertising IDs, no third-party trackers. We don't measure you because we don't need to.

The one exception: to manage an optional subscription, Apple and our payment provider process an anonymous purchase identifier. That's the only thing that ever leaves your device — and it's never linked to your personal content. Each app's privacy policy spells this out in plain language.

Questions

Good to know

Does SuiteForge collect my personal data?

No. SuiteForge apps store everything on your device, optionally synced through your own private iCloud. There are no SuiteForge accounts, no analytics SDKs and no third-party trackers. The only data that leaves your device is the anonymous purchase information Apple and our subscription provider need to manage a subscription.

Do I need an account?

No. SuiteForge apps work immediately on first launch — no sign-up, no email, no password.

What kinds of apps does SuiteForge make?

Focused, single-purpose apps in two areas: Health & Wellness (intermittent fasting, morning routines, step tracking) and Productivity (relationship and life organisation). Each app does one thing well and runs entirely on-device.

Are the apps free?

Each app is free to download and use, with an optional subscription that unlocks advanced features. You can use the core of every app without paying.

How do I get help or suggest a feature?

Every app has a Feedback section in its settings, or you can email suiteforgeops@gmail.com directly. We read everything.
