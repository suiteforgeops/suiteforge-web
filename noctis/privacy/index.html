SuiteForge/Noctis/Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Noctis · Last updated 14 November 2026

The short version. Noctis stores your data on your device only. It does not sync to any cloud. There is no Noctis account. We do not use analytics or advertising trackers, and we never sell your data. Purchases are handled by Apple; nothing else leaves your device.

1. Who we are

Noctis is an iPhone app published by Nicolas Dojcilovic under the name SuiteForge, who is the data controller for the processing described below (see the legal notice). For any privacy question, contact us at suiteforgeops@gmail.com.

2. Data stored on your device

Everything you create in Noctis is stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device frameworks. Noctis does not sync your data to iCloud or to any other cloud service, and we do not operate any server that stores your personal content.

Your nights and audio extracts

Each night's measurements (sound level, noisy breathing, snoring, breathing rate), your notes and evening habits, and the audio extracts are stored only on your device. They are never sent to SuiteForge or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Sound analysis on your iPhone

Noisy breathing is detected by Noctis's own signal processing, and snoring is confirmed by Apple's built-in sound classifier (SoundAnalysis). Both run on your iPhone, with no server and no third-party service.

3. Permissions Noctis may request

Microphone

Noctis uses the microphone only during a night you start, to measure noisy breathing and, while the app stays open with the phone face down, snoring. The sound is analysed on your iPhone as it comes in and is not kept, except short audio extracts of notable moments (only when the phone lies face down), saved in the app so you can replay them. No sound is ever uploaded. Without the microphone, Noctis cannot measure your night.

Apple Health (optional, Premium)

If you turn on Health sync in Settings, Noctis asks for permission to save each night's sleep period and average breathing rate to Apple Health, and to read your sleep analysis. This version of Noctis does not read any Health data. This data stays in Apple Health on your devices and is never sent to SuiteForge.

Notifications (optional)

An evening bedtime reminder, if you turn it on, scheduled locally on your iPhone. Noctis sends no other notifications.

Each permission is optional, is requested only when relevant, and can be changed at any time in the iOS Settings app.

4. Subscriptions and purchases

Noctis offers an optional auto-renewing subscription and an optional one-time lifetime purchase. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple. Noctis checks your purchases on your device with Apple's StoreKit framework and uses no third-party purchase service: no purchase information is sent to SuiteForge or to anyone other than Apple. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not require or create an account.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data.

6. Your rights

Because your content is stored on your own device, you remain in control of it at all times: you can edit or delete it within the app, and removing the app removes its local data. As Noctis is published from France, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies to the limited purchase-related processing described above; you may exercise your applicable rights of access, rectification, erasure and objection by contacting suiteforgeops@gmail.com.

7. Children

Noctis is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new "last updated" date.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email suiteforgeops@gmail.com.
