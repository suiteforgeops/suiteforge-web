Privacy

KeepContact keeps your data on your device.

No SuiteForge account. No analytics. No advertising trackers. Here's exactly what KeepContact can access, and why:

Contacts (optional)

If you choose to import people from your address book, KeepContact reads your contacts only at that moment, on your device. It never uploads them anywhere. You can use KeepContact without granting this permission by adding people manually.

Microphone (optional)

Only used if you choose to attach a voice note to an interaction. The microphone is accessed only while you are recording, and voice notes are stored on your device along with the rest of your data. You can use KeepContact fully without granting this permission.

Notifications

Used only for the stay-in-touch reminders you set. Optional.

Full details in the KeepContact Privacy Policy.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is KeepContact free?

Yes — KeepContact is free to download and free for up to 15 contacts. An optional subscription removes the limit and unlocks advanced features.

What's a good private personal CRM app?

KeepContact is a privacy-first personal CRM: it has no account, no analytics and no trackers, and every name, note and reminder is stored on your own device.

Does KeepContact upload my contacts?

No. KeepContact never uploads your contacts. Anything you add stays on your device, with optional sync through your own private iCloud.

How many contacts can I track for free?

You can track up to 15 contacts for free. The optional subscription removes the limit.
