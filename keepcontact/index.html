Privacy

KeepContact keeps your data on your device.

No SuiteForge account. No analytics. No advertising trackers. Here is exactly what KeepContact can access, and why.

  • No ads, no analytics, no trackers
  • Every permission is optional
  • Nothing stored on our servers

Full details in the KeepContact Privacy Policy.

Contacts (optional)

If you choose to import people from your address book, KeepContact reads your contacts on your device to import them and to suggest updates. It never uploads them anywhere and never writes to your address book. You can use KeepContact without granting this permission by adding people manually.

Calendar (optional)

If you turn on calendar suggestions, KeepContact reads your past events on your device to suggest logging the time you spent with your contacts. Nothing is saved without your OK, and nothing leaves your iPhone.

Camera (optional)

Only used when you scan a business card, to read it and prefill a contact. The image is neither saved nor sent.

Microphone (optional)

Only used if you choose to record a voice note on an interaction. The microphone is accessed only while you are recording. Voice notes and their transcripts are created and stored on your device.

Notifications (optional)

Used only for the stay-in-touch reminders, birthday notifications and the morning brief you choose to receive. They are scheduled locally on your iPhone.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is KeepContact free?

Yes. KeepContact is free to download, and you can track up to 15 contacts and 3 reminders for free. KeepContact Pro removes the limits and unlocks the on-device AI, voice notes, the morning brief, the health score, circles, statistics, messages to several people and encrypted backups. Your year in review, every December, is free. Subscriptions come with a 7-day free trial, or you can get Pro for life with a single payment.

What's a good private personal CRM app?

KeepContact is a privacy-first personal CRM for iPhone: no account, no server, no analytics and no trackers. Every name, note and reminder is stored on your device, and its App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected".

Does KeepContact upload my contacts?

No. KeepContact never uploads your contacts or notes. It works 100% offline and everything stays on your iPhone. Backups are files you save yourself, and can be encrypted with a password only you know.

How does the AI work without sending my data?

KeepContact uses Apple's on-device AI model. Summaries, message ideas and the facts it picks up from your notes are generated on your iPhone; your notes never leave it. It requires an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence; the rest of the app works on any iPhone running a supported iOS version.

Does KeepContact sync with iCloud?

Not at the moment. KeepContact keeps everything on your iPhone. To move your data to another iPhone, KeepContact Pro includes a backup in Settings, which you can encrypt with your own password.
