Why SuiteForge

I make apps to create, learn and be useful.

Nicolas Dojcilovic, founder

Who's behind SuiteForge

SuiteForge is me, Nicolas Dojcilovic. I live near Paris, I'm Franco-Serbian, and I spent 25 years in banking before I started making iPhone apps. I'm not a developer by training: I learned by building.

Why I make apps

To create, to have fun and to learn, while being useful. A few years ago I decided to stop putting barriers in my own way. I passed the motorcycle licence I had dreamed of since I was a child, then explored one field after another: real estate, personal finance, personal development, health, and making apps. It woke my brain up, and I love it.

You can make and learn a lot of things at any age. Each app is my way of doing that, and of being useful to someone along the way.

Apps born from real life

I make the apps I need, for me and my family, then I make them good enough for everyone.

  • Ježko: My daughter is 10. She understands Serbian when her grandparents speak, but she doesn't dare answer. I didn't find an app made for her, so I made Ježko: one story, one hedgehog, ten minutes a day.
  • Crescendo: I go to the gym at least three times a week, in short 30-minute sessions. Crescendo started as my own lifting log: I wanted it to tell me what to put on the bar, and why.
  • RoutineFlow: I use RoutineFlow every morning. It walks me through my routine and keeps my phone out of the way until I'm done.
  • WalkQuest: I've never seen myself as sporty, but I try to stay active: when I stop moving, my back lets me know in the morning. My floor is 5,000 steps a day. WalkQuest gives those steps somewhere to go.

KeepContact and FastClear follow the same rule: one need, one app, nothing more.

What I promise

I make apps I'd want my own family to use. So there is no account, no ads and no tracker, and your data stays on your device or in your own iCloud. Each app's privacy policy says exactly what happens, in plain language. How SuiteForge handles your data.

Talk to me

A bug, an idea, a question: I read every message, at suiteforgeops@gmail.com.
